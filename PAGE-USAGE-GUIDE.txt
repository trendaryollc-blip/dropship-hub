DROPSHIPHUB — PAGE WALKTHROUGH GUIDE
====================================================

This document explains how to use the four main work surfaces of the app:

  1. Dashboard          (/dashboard)
  2. Find Products      (/products)
  3. Find Suppliers     (/suppliers)
  4. Product Detail     (/products/[id])

Each section describes WHAT the feature does, WHY it exists, and the exact
click-by-click workflow to get value from it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DASHBOARD  (/dashboard)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The dashboard is your daily command center. It loads live data from your
connected stores (Firestore) and the product discovery feed (CJ-backed),
refreshes roughly every 60 seconds, and degrades gracefully: if a source
fails you will see a banner like "showing the last available data" instead
of fake zeros. If everything fails, you get a real error card with a
"Try Again" button.

TOP OF PAGE

- "Customize" button (eye icon, top right):
  Opens a panel listing every dashboard section with a checkbox:
    Command Center, Revenue & Profit, Insights, Product Discovery,
    Supplier Network, Order Operations, Market Intelligence,
    Store Operations, Growth Tools.
  Uncheck sections you do not want; they are hidden on this device only.
  "Show all sections" (reset icon) brings everything back. Preferences are
  stored in localStorage. A "{n} hidden" badge tells you how many are off.

- Status banners (amber, under the customize row):
    - "Couldn't reach the live data feed — showing the last successful data"
      + Retry button: the app is offline-ish; your numbers are the last
      good fetch, not live.
    - "Store data / product feed unavailable" variants: one upstream
      (Firestore or the CJ discovery feed) failed. Retry to re-pull.

SECTION: COMMAND CENTER (HeroCommandCenter)

- Greeting header with your name, time-aware greeting, and a
  "{n} saved" bookmark chip that jumps to /saved.
- Business Health Score ring (0-100): green >= 80 "Running strong",
  amber >= 60 "Needs attention", red "Critical". Hover or tap it for an
  explanation of what feeds the score (stores, revenue, orders, product
  activity). If the score is under 80 you get a "Connect Store" link.
- SmartSearchBar: a command-style search input. Type a product or topic,
  use quick-action chips, voice input, or a suggestion link. It routes you
  into product search or the AI assistant.
- Quick Actions grid, four tiles:
    Find Products -> /products (badge shows trending count)
    Suppliers     -> /suppliers (badge shows online supplier count)
    Calculator    -> /calculator
    AI Tools      -> /ai

SECTION: REVENUE & PROFIT (RevenueProfitHub)

- Status badges: "{n} stores", "{n} suppliers", "{n} pending",
  and a pulsing "Your data" badge (totals come from your Firestore).
- KPI cards: Revenue (with growth % chip), Orders, Avg Order Value.
- "Revenue Overview" bar chart: hover bars for $ values per date;
  first/middle/last date labels on the axis; empty state "No revenue data
  yet". "Full Report" link -> /revenue.
- "Profit Tracker" card: Profit per Order, Margin % progress bar
  (hover for the profit-margin explanation tooltip), AOV, Total Profit,
  Total Revenue, Total Orders. "View Tracker" -> /profit-tracker.
Workflow: scan the trend and margin first; if margin is low, jump to the
Profit Tracker.

SECTION: INSIGHTS (AIIntelligenceHub)

- AI Daily Pick card: today's scored top product with:
    - ScoreRing score, category/platform chips, "Expires {date}",
      yesterday's result ("Yesterday: won/lost"), risk chip.
    - "Source Price", "Est. sell price" (rule: ~2.5x source + $4.99),
      "Est. margin", "Orders/mo" estimate.
    - "Why we scored this" checklist, "Earnings Preview"
      (profit/order, orders/mo, est. monthly revenue),
      saturation bar.
    - Actions: "Start Selling" -> /products, "Compare" ->
      /calculator/profit prefilled, "Source" -> the CJ listing.
- Market Briefing card: Opps/Risks/Trends tiles, sentiment %, up to two
  insight lines, last-scan date, "Full Insights" -> /ai.
- Smart Alerts card: unread count, "Mark all read", up to 4 alerts;
  click one to mark it read and go to its action link (default
  /monitoring). "View All Alerts" -> /monitoring.

SECTION: PRODUCT DISCOVERY

- "Trending Products" carousel with left/right arrows (320px steps);
  "View All" -> /products.
- Each trending card: platform chip, trend chip (+x% vs category average),
  sparkline, price, Est. Margin, Est. score (a heuristic badge, not a
  guarantee), est. demand/competition chips, "competitors" list,
  "whyTrending" line.
- Per-card actions:
    "+"        -> add to the quick compare tray (max 4, no duplicates)
    "Save"     -> toggle bookmark (localStorage + account sync)
    "View Product" -> opens /products/{id} for that product
- Shortcut tiles beneath: Saved -> /saved, Validation ->
  /product-validation, Listings -> /product-listings, Lifecycle ->
  /product-lifecycle.

QUICK COMPARE BAR (floating, bottom)

- Appears once you add products via the "+" button on discovery cards.
- Shows up to 4 items with thumbnail, name, price, margin; hover an X to
  remove; dashed "+ Add product" tile -> /products when fewer than 4.
- "Compare" button (needs 2+) -> /products?compare=... for the full
  comparison view. Trash icon clears all.

SECTION: SUPPLIER NETWORK

- "Network Status" card: total suppliers, Online/Busy/Offline counts,
  "All Suppliers" -> /suppliers, "SRM" -> /srm.
- "Top Suppliers" card: up to 4 rows with trust badge, status dot,
  location, response time ("n/a" when unwired), rating, completion rate.
  A row links to /suppliers. "Performance" -> /supplier-performance.

SECTION: ORDER OPERATIONS

- "Fulfillment Pipeline" card: four stage bars (Pending, Processing,
  Shipped, Delivered) with counts, link "View Pipeline" -> /fulfillment,
  and a Recent Orders list (customer, product, amount, status, time).
- "Pipeline Revenue" card: total revenue, total profit, margin %.
- Quick Actions: Bulk Orders, Returns, Order Router, Shipping Optimizer.

SECTION: MARKET INTELLIGENCE

- "Market Heatmap" card: "{n} overheating / trending up / cooling"
  chips, up to 8 category tiles colored by heat, estimated margin per
  category, trend arrow. "Full Map" -> /products.
- "Market Pulse" card: price rows with ±% vs category average and
  sparkline. "Price vs category average" tooltips.
- Competitive Tools: Competitor Analysis, Price War Tracker, Reports.

SECTION: STORE OPERATIONS

- Four link cards: My Stores (/store), Multi-Store (/multi-store),
  Monitoring (/monitoring), Store Health (/health). The first shows how
  many stores are connected.

SECTION: GROWTH & TOOLS

- Six tiles: Daily Missions (/missions), Calculator (/calculator),
  Trend Predictor (/trends), Customer Service (/customer-service),
  Ad ROI (/ad-roi), Daily Digest (/digest).

FOOTER

- Market Ticker bar: horizontally scrollable price/platform rows;
  no sparkline by design.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. FIND PRODUCTS  (/products)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two modes: discovery (you have not searched yet) and results (you have).

SEARCHHEADER (top)

- Search box: type a full natural-language query, e.g. "wireless earbuds
  under $15 with high margin". The app parses it into filters.
- Platform chips: choose which marketplaces to search (Amazon, eBay,
  Walmart, Temu, Shein, Etsy, AliExpress, etc.). "Search All Platforms"
  toggles them on.
- Recent searches dropdown: re-run or clear past queries.
- Filters button: opens the FilterPanel (drawer on mobile).
- Ask AI input: type "Help me find winning products" style prompts that
  hand off to the AI assistant.
- Voice button: dictate your search.
- Visual search button (camera): upload a photo of a product (JPG/PNG/WebP,
  <= 10 MB) and the app identifies it and searches for it. Requires
  sign-in; errors if the file is wrong type/too big or unidentifiable.

DISCOVERY SECTIONS (before you search)

- PersonalizedRecommendations: recommendations based on your history
  (signed-in users).
- AICollections: AI-curated sets of products.
- TrendingSection: hot products right now.
- NichesSection: profitable niches worth exploring.
- CategoriesSection: browse by category.
- HowItWorksSection: short explanation of the search-and-validate flow.

SEARCH RESULTS

- ResultsHeader: "{n} results", sort dropdown (relevance, price, margin,
  etc.), grid/list view toggle, filter button with active-filter count.
- PlatformProgress: live per-platform loading/error/success while
  streaming results. If a platform key is missing or a scrape fails, it
  lists it under "platform errors".
- "Incomplete results" warning if some platforms failed — you may not be
  seeing everything.
- QuickActionChips (after a search with results): one-tap prompts such as
  Validate, Find Suppliers, Generate Listings, Analyze Competitors,
  Find Similar, Calculate Profit, Compare, Create Alert, Ask AI.
- Each product card (EnrichedProductCard or ListItemCard):
    - Image, title (2-line clamp), source platform chip, price.
    - Heuristic badges clearly marked "est.": Golden Score ring, trend
      chip, saturation badge, est. margin. Treat them as starting
      hypotheses, not measurements.
    - Save/like toggle.
    - Select checkbox for bulk actions and compare.
    - Per-card AI Actions menu: Analyze, Find Suppliers, Generate
      Listing, Validate.
    - "Push to Store" (if a store is connected).
    - "Order Sample" opens the sample-order modal.
- Compare: select up to 4 cards -> "Compare" -> /products?compare=...
  view with side-by-side table.
- Search Alerts: "Create Alert" (from the AI chips or the alert modal)
  for the current query with a type — new product, price drop, or any
  change — and it notifies you when new matches appear.
- Empty states:
    - No products found: "Try a different search query or enable more
      platforms" + Ask-AI fallback + suggestion chips.
    - "No products match your filters": Clear all filters.
- Back-to-top floating button after scrolling ~500px.

HOW TO USE IT WELL (typical workflow)

1. Start on discovery: skim Trending, Niches, Categories, AICollections.
2. Search a niche with natural language; use voice/image search if you
   have a product photo or idea.
3. Narrow with Filters (brand, price, rating, margin, competition,
   trend, platform), then sort.
4. Read a card's est. badges, save interesting ones, add notes.
5. Quick Actions: Validate, Find Suppliers, Generate Listing, Analyze
   Competitors, Calculate Profit, Compare, Create Alert.
6. Select 2-4 products for the Compare view.
7. Click a product card to open its Product Detail page.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. FIND SUPPLIERS  (/suppliers)  — "Supplier Intelligence"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tabbed page, three tabs: Discover, Intel, SRM. Tab state is in the URL
(?tab=discover|intel|srm).

DISCOVER TAB

- Product context banner: when you arrive from a product page (query
  params product/category/source/price), you get "Finding suppliers for
  <product>" with chips, and results are ranked against that product.
  "Clear context" resets.
- Search bar: "Search suppliers by name, category, or location...";
  debounced, Enter runs the search, X clears, Esc blurs, mic button
  for voice.
- Platform chips: toggle which supplier platforms to query (Alibaba,
  DHgate, Global Sources, AliExpress, CJ, Amazon, Google, Walmart,
  Other). A "no key" badge means that platform is not configured.
- Ask AI toggle: natural-language supplier search panel; it runs a
  keyword search across your supplier catalog (disclaimer shown).
- Filters button (badge = active count): opens SupplierFilterPanel —
  trust badge (gold/silver/bronze), location chips, min rating, shipping
  speed, specialization chips, price competitiveness, certifications,
  and active-filter chips with X-to-remove.
- Sort: Top Rated, Most Reliable, Fastest Response, Most Orders,
  Best Price. View toggle grid/list.
- Results: SupplierCard (list) or SupplierGridCard — avatar, trust
  badge, source chip, location/flag, response time, shipping days,
  specialization chips (+N), reliability ScoreRing, star rating, price
  competitiveness bar, orders/mo, MOQ, free-shipping threshold,
  AI actions menu (Analyze Supplier, Find Their Products, Start
  Negotiation, Check Performance, Request Sample), "View store" hint,
  Target (focus) button, compare checkbox (max 4).
- Compare: with 2+ selected, a bottom "Compare N Suppliers" bar opens
  a ComparisonModal table — rating, reliability, response, shipping,
  completion, dispute rate, monthly orders, quality, communication,
  price competitiveness, with best-in-emerald and "Best" tags, plus
  "View Details" links. "AI Deep Compare" opens /ai?q= with a
  comparison prompt.
- Focus (Target): pins a banner with the focused supplier's summary and
  a "View Full Profile" button.
- SupplierQuickActions row (when you have results): Analyze Suppliers,
  Find Their Products, Start Negotiation, Compare Pricing, Request
  Samples, Deep Performance Check — each produces a card above the list
  with a rendered answer, Copy button, Retry, and "Open in AI" link.
- When zero results: SupplierAISuggestions (matched from your product
  portfolio, rules-based) and Smart Supplier Collections (Top
  Performers, Fast Shippers, Budget Friendly, New & Rising, Verified
  Gold, Free Shipping).

INTEL TAB

- Overview: supplier score cards (status pill, reliability counter,
  refund rate, avg shipping, complaints, stock reliability, trend
  arrows) and a reliability-trend line chart per supplier ("No snapshot
  data available yet" when empty).
- Alerts: severity-coded cards (quality degradation, shipping delay,
  stock low, refund spike, communication issue) with metric
  previous -> current and % change.
- Comparison: a sortable supplier table (reliability, refund rate,
  shipping days, complaints, stock reliability, price score, orders)
  with the best value highlighted.

SRM TAB (Supplier Relationship Management)

- KPI cards: Supplier Score, Active Negotiations, Unread Messages,
  Auto-Switch Rules.
- Scorecards: per-supplier letter grade (A+-F), overall score bar,
  trend, last-evaluated date, expandable criteria (Speed, Quality,
  Communication, Price, Reliability with weights) and a 14-point
  score-history sparkline.
- Messages: sent/incoming chips, type (inquiry/negotiation/order issue/
  quality/general), status (sent/read/error), CJ ID when available.
  Compose via the "Compose Message" button.
- Negotiations: status chip (active/accepted/rejected/expired/
  counter-offered), Initial -> Current -> Target price, progress bar,
  expandable round-by-round thread (You vs Supplier bubbles), inline
  add-round form, Accept/Reject actions.
- Auto-Switch: rules like "if metric < threshold, then alert / auto-
  switch / notify"; add form (supplier, metric, threshold, action),
  delete rules, switch history log (from -> to, reason, trigger type).

SUPPLIER DETAIL PAGE  (/suppliers/[id])

- Hero: avatar, name, trust badge, source chip, location, rating,
  reliability ring (or "not measured").
- CTAs: "Visit Supplier" (external), "Browse Products" (CJ only),
  "Contact Supplier" (slide-over form; posts to /api/suppliers/contact;
  outbound delivery is not wired yet).
- Quick stats: Rating, Reliability, Response, Completion, Disputes,
  Orders/mo (— when unmeasured).
- Sticky tabs: Overview, Performance, Scorecard, SRM, AI Assistant.
    - Overview: Due Diligence panel (generate a report -> risk level
      verdict Recommended/Use Caution/Avoid, red flags, strengths,
      history analysis, best-for/avoid-for), About, Shipping &
      Fulfillment, Quality & Trust, Product Catalog, Communication.
    - Performance: status badge, 4 metric cards with trend arrows,
      stock reliability bar, trend charts with % change.
    - Scorecard: big grade letter, trend, 5-axis radar chart,
      per-criteria score bars with weights, score history.
    - SRM: Negotiations (bubbles), Messages (inline composer, status
      chips), Auto-Switch (rule toggles, trigger count, fallback).
    - AI Assistant: contextual to this supplier; quick tiles (Analyze,
      Find Products, Draft Negotiation, Deep Check, Request Samples,
      Compare Pricing), chat transcript, Enter-to-send.

HOW TO USE IT WELL

1. From a product page, click "Find Suppliers" — you land with product
   context pre-filled.
2. Pick your platforms; watch PlatformProgress while results stream in.
3. Filter (trust, rating, shipping, specialization, price), sort, switch
   grid/list.
4. Focus a supplier, then compare 2-4 with the comparison modal.
5. Run a Quick Action (Analyze/Negotiate/Samples) to get an AI read.
6. Open the supplier detail page for Due Diligence, Performance,
   Scorecard, and SRM history.
7. Use Intel tab to monitor trends/alerts; use SRM tab to run
   negotiations, log messages, and set auto-switch rules.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. PRODUCT DETAIL  (/products/[id])
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Data is bootstrapped from sessionStorage ("selectedProduct") or from URL
query params (t, p, img, link, src, r, rev, cat, tags) — so links from
the dashboard/discovery/search are shareable and re-runnable. It fires
background calls to enrich images, reviews, market intel, and listing
suggestions. If there is no product and no title param, you get
"Product not found" with a "Back to Search" link.

HERO SHOWCASE

- Image gallery: main image (click for lightbox with prev/next, arrows,
  Escape to close, thumbnail strip, counter "n / N"); spinner while
  extra images load; empty state shows a package icon.
- Source platform tag (colored pill), product title, category badge,
  up to 4 tags, product ID (barcode-style monospace).
- Price hero: "Listed Price" big number (only when price > 0).
- Quick stats: rating pill, reviews pill, "Risk: n/100" badge
  (green <= 30, amber <= 60, red above), source pill.
- Rating badge with stars, count, and a "Top Rated" shield when
  rating >= 4.5.
- Primary CTA: "View on {platform}" when the real link exists;
  otherwise "Open {platform} store" or "Search {platform} for this
  product", with a gray note that the direct link is unavailable.

PRODUCTACTIONBAR (desktop toolbar / mobile bottom bar)

- Save (heart): syncs to your saved products (local + account, cap 500).
- Compare: scrolls to the price-comparison section.
- Suppliers: /suppliers pre-filtered for this product.
- Order Sample (green): modal form — name, phone, address, city, state,
  zip, country — posts to /api/orders/sample; success view "Order
  Placed!" auto-closes.
- Start Selling (blue): modal listing your connected stores; pick one,
  "Push to Store & Start Selling" posts to /api/orders/start-selling;
  empty state links to /store to connect one.
- Monitor (amber): adds this product to price monitoring; turns green
  "Monitoring" once done.
- Validate (amber): opens /product-validation pre-filled with this
  product in a new tab.
- Platform external link.
- Analyze (desktop): scrolls to the calculator section.

NEXT STEPS grid

- Find Suppliers, Calculate Profit, Check Competitors, Validate Product
  — each pre-fills its target page with this product's data.

SECTIONS (scroll nav on the right, mobile tab bar; active highlight)

1. Price Comparison (#price-comparison)
   - No/single platform: shows the listed price and a "Use Calculator"
     link.
   - Multi-platform table: Lowest price + Savings header; columns
     Platform, Price, Rating, Trend (sparkline or "no history" em-dash),
     Stock (In/Out/Unknown dot), Savings vs listed. Cheapest row is
     "Best Deal", highest-rated non-cheapest is "Most Trusted".

2. Profit Calculator (#calculator)
   - Presets: Amazon FBA (15% fee, $3.5 ship, $5 ads), Shopify
     Dropship (0%, $5, $8), eBay Resell (13%, $4, $2).
   - Inputs: Source Price, Selling Price, Platform Fee %, Shipping,
     Ad Spend per sale.
   - Results: Net Profit, ROI %, Break Even units, Profit Margin bar
     with label ("Excellent" >= 30, "Good" >= 15, "Low — optimize").
   - "Open Full Calculator" keeps your numbers via query params.

3. Market Intelligence (#market-intel)
   - Trend direction tile, search-interest index (0-100) bar, seasonality
     + best time to sell, competition level + price-war risk,
     "Can You Compete?" callout, risk ScoreRing with factor pills.
   - Every tile is labeled as an estimate, not a market measurement.

4. Review Intelligence (#reviews)
   - Rating hero (avg stars, count), 1-5 star distribution bars
     (labeled when estimated), sentiment cards (positive/neutral/
     negative with quote snippets), top keywords, common praise and
     complaints lists, trustworthiness banner (n/100).

5. Best Supplier Matches (#suppliers)
   - Ranked shortlist (gold/silver/bronze) with name, trust badge,
     location, price, shipping days, response time, reliability ring.
   - "View All" -> /suppliers filtered to this product.

6. Listing Optimization (#listings)
   - Suggested title, description, tags (each with copy button),
     suggested price range, platform-specific tip card.

7. Similar Products (#similar)
   - Grid of similar items (image, title, price, platform, rating) and
     "Frequently Bought Together" list; each opens the external listing
     or the normalized product URL.

8. Related Searches (#searches)
   - Tag-derived query pills that link to /products?q=...

STICKYPRODUCTBAR

- After you scroll past the hero, a compact bar slides in with thumbnail,
  title, price, rating, reviews, and the same CTA label as the hero
  ("View on {platform}" etc.).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CROSS-CUTTING NOTES (what is NOT there / honesty guardrails)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Many numbers are explicitly labeled "est." or "heuristic" because the
  underlying APIs (CJ shipping, price history, Google Trends) are not
  wired end-to-end. Treat them as directional.
- The product detail page has no dedicated share button; share by
  copying the URL (it re-hydrates from query params).
- Supplier chat/sample-order widgets, health monitor, seasonal calendar,
  price-intel table, radar compare, niche discovery, and community
  reviews components exist in code but are not mounted on a page yet.
- /platforms redirects to /admin/platforms; it is not linked from the
  main nav.
- If any section fails to load (network, API key missing), each section
  shows an amber warning card with its own "Retry" button — nothing is
  silently zero-filled.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ADDENDUM — NEWLY WIRED (previously unwired/dead, now functional)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Supplier Contact ("Contact Supplier" slide-over): now actually delivers.
  Enter the supplier's email in the new "Supplier's Email (for delivery)"
  field (or store contactEmail on the supplier record); the inquiry is
  emailed via the configured provider (Resend, SendGrid fallback) and the
  success screen tells you whether it was Sent, Logged, or Failed.
- SRM Messages: outbound messages are sent via the CJ message API for
  CJ suppliers; for any other supplier, pass supplierEmail and the
  message is emailed. Status (sent/failed/logged) is stored per message.
- SRM Negotiations: new rounds and new negotiations are emailed to the
  supplier when supplierEmail is supplied.
- Suppliers page has a 4th tab, "Tools": Health (monitor + alerts),
  Smart Match (4-step wizard), Seasonal (calendar), Price Intel
  (ranked offers + margin bar), Radar (spider compare), Niches
  (opportunity discovery).
- Supplier detail page has a 6th tab, "Tools": Community Reviews,
  Sample Orders, Supplier Chat.
- Product detail Trend column: every view records a same-day-deduped
  per-platform price snapshot (users/{uid}/productPriceViews);
  /api/products/price-history serves it; the Trend cells render real
  sparklines once 2+ points exist, and a "Price history" button opens
  the PriceHistoryChart modal (trend %, low/avg/high).
- /platforms is now a real page: selling channels with live Connected/
  Not connected status from your store connections, sourcing platforms,
  and a search-all-platforms shortcut — no more admin redirect.
- Find Products discovery: returning visitors see SmartFeed (Recommended
  for You + Recent Searches with re-run and clear-history).
- BulkActionsBar is mounted on results: multi-select cards via the card
  checkbox, then Export CSV or Bulk Push to Store (real authenticated
  store fetch + push with per-product success/fail counts).
- BulkActionsBar auth fixed: Firebase Bearer token is now sent on the
  connections fetch and every bulk push (previously would 401).
